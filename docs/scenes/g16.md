## s073 - Fort Rajin and the Two-Front War

- **Where / speakers:** Fort Rajin and fighting on the eastern plain. Named participants include General Bronson, Weber, Wallace, Tippi, and Luise, alongside guards, Rolandian soldiers, a merchant, and the player-named hero.
- **War planning:** Burnstein will attack from the east while its ally Ranzak passes through the southern forest to strike Rolandia’s rear. Dividing the defenders or retreating is rejected as too dangerous.
- **Counterplan:** The party proposes making the allied armies fight each other. An ancient Growsian device can turn the forest into the Lost Forest and misdirect Ranzak’s troops toward Burnstein.
- **Relationships and abilities:** Luise is explicitly identified as a Growsian, allowing her companions to avoid the forest’s effects. Her mother, Sandra, has the needed magic crystal; Tippi contacts Sandra as “Master.” Bronson promises to unlock the ruins.
- **Other wartime dialogue:** Later lines praise the operation’s success, describe changing alliances and the exposed false king, and express growing confidence in the party. Soldiers discuss defenses, supplies, and the ruins; a merchant sells equipment and meals.
- **Major confrontation:** A powerful enemy admits exchanging magical instruction for shelter, then turning on his former associates. He credits a Grosh crystal obtained through Sandra’s daughter with restoring his abilities, claims to surpass Gevel, and demonstrates rapid regeneration.
- **Outcome:** The party removes that regenerative advantage but fails to finish him: an earthquake, described as the Power Stone’s backlash, lets him teleport away. Weber will inform Ranzak, Tippi reports to Sandra, and the party plans to notify other countries and the Imperial Knights.
- **Type / tone:** Primarily main-story military planning and a major battle, mixed with changing NPC dialogue and shop menus. Urgent and strategic, with angry confrontation, renewed hope, and Tippi’s comic reprimands.

- **Type / tone:** Main-story dungeon and battle material, including puzzle prompts, machinery readouts, treasure, and healing messages. Urgent, technical, and increasingly suspicious.
