## s000 - Awakening and the First Journey

- **Setting:** The hero’s home in the royal capital, including bedrooms and the downstairs family gathering. The file also contains a dream sequence.
- **Speakers:** Luise, Tippi, the hero’s adoptive mother—the court magician identified elsewhere here as Sandra—and an unnamed mercenary and Sierra in the dream. The player chooses the hero’s responses.
- **Family and companion:** Luise calls the hero her older brother. Her mother introduces Tippi as a magically created homunculus with a fairy’s appearance. Luise attends the Magic Academy and will undertake practical training under her mother.
- **Central revelation:** Sandra adopted the abandoned hero seventeen years ago. Divinations showed two radically different futures: he could destroy the world or become its saving light. She kept him within the capital while raising him to choose rightly; now she permits him to travel and gain experience.
- **Shopping and assessment:** The hero receives 75 Elm for preparations. Afterward, Tippi evaluates his conduct through numerous alternative responses covering judgment, speed, insight, attentiveness, knowledge, honesty, reliability, and kindness. A system option allows the shopping sequence and its recorded actions to be reset.
- **First destination:** The hero describes dream ruins with a distant tower-like rock. Sandra suggests a cape northwest of the capital. Tippi will accompany him; Luise must remain for training. Departure dialogue includes two paralysis balls and a possible gift of 30 Elm from Luise.
- **Dream and awakening:** An unnamed mercenary offers Sierra his treasured, strangely luminous ring and proposes marriage; she accepts. Luise then tries to wake the hero, and Tippi succeeds with a kick. The excerpt does not identify the dream couple’s relationship to him.
- **Type and tone:** Main-story opening with character assessment, household interactions, and system prompts. Affectionate family scenes and Tippi’s abrasive comedy surround serious questions of destiny. Explicit event-checkpoint jump prompts are debug text.

## s001 - Rosaria’s Streets and Early Leads

- **Setting:** Rosaria, capital of Rolandia: castle gates, city exits, shops, the South Avenue shopping district, and the LLM magic classroom. Dialogue includes several different story states.
- **Speakers:** Tippi, Luise, and unnamed guards, soldiers, citizens, children, merchants, shopkeepers, and a magic instructor. Sandra, Wallace, and Ariost are discussed.
- **Finding the Featherians:** The party seeks a way to reach the Featherians living high on a rock; Tippi cannot fly that far. Citizens mention sightings from the cape and a bespectacled scholar at the inn. Later lines suggest finding Ariost at the Magic Academy.
- **Castle incidents:** Guards and townspeople discuss a castle intruder and an attack on Sandra. One guard thanks the hero for defeating an intruder and requests discretion. Access to Sandra’s laboratory is denied because ordinary citizens cannot enter the castle, even in an emergency.
- **Following a suspect:** Children argue over a masked man seen leaving the castle. Questioning them reveals that he ran toward the east gate, also the direction of Wallace’s village. An eastern gate guard separately recalls someone leaving hurriedly during the night.
- **Town life:** Shops reopen after renovations, while a collapsed eastern bridge disrupts supplies and access to the academy until repaired. Optional conversations include dating couples, hiding children, an unusual cramped shop, and encouragement for Sandra’s son.
- **Tutorial material:** An optional lesson explains the status screen, equipment, experience, HP/MP, combat statistics, attack recovery, and elemental resistance. Notices also advertise events and recommend the configuration menu.
- **Type and tone:** Main-story investigation mixed with town side interactions, shopping interfaces, and tutorials. Mostly conversational and comic, with growing anxiety over failures in castle security; no sustained battle scene appears.

## s002 - A Rescued Princess and Elliot’s Resemblance

- **Setting:** Rosaria’s streets, shops, gates, and castle approach, across later story states involving Sandra’s recovery, wartime duties, and Princess Leticia’s return.
- **Speakers:** Tippi, Luise, Sandra, Leticia, Elliot, Xenos, unnamed companions, attackers, guards, and townspeople. Oswald appears in the speaker labels; Misha is named as one of the companions assigned to guard the others.
- **Changing circumstances:** Citizens acknowledge that medicine was found for Sandra and welcome her recovery. Other dialogue recognizes the hero’s entry into royal service and knighthood, asks him to rescue prisoners, and expresses fear of war and conscription.
- **Leticia’s rescue:** On returning safely, Leticia asks whether the party rescued her only because her father ordered it. Choices distinguish royal orders, civic duty, and friendship. In the friendship branch, companions affirm that they would have helped without orders, and she thanks them warmly.
- **Attack on Elliot:** The party encounters men pursuing Elliot. One admits they were hired to kill him, and another tells him to stop talking. The party intervenes; afterward, the hero and Luise pursue the masked man while companions remain to protect the others. The pursuit fails.
- **Xenos and Karen:** During the search, Xenos discusses his sister Karen’s recovery after another bandit attack and hospitalization in Rachelle. Hoping a reward might cover her medical expenses, he sets off to pursue the suspect.
- **Unresolved identity mystery:** Leticia says Elliot closely resembles Richard, Burnstein’s newly crowned king, and wears an identical bracelet. Elliot says his parents sent him to Rolandia Castle, but he lost his introduction letter. The party discusses possible explanations without establishing any; Elliot joins them, and they propose continuing at the castle.
- **Type and tone:** Main-story rescue aftermath, battle dialogue, pursuit, and an identity mystery, interspersed with ordinary town and shop text. Relief and friendship give way to danger, suspicion, and confusion.

## s003 - Wartime Supply Trouble and a Claimed Brother

- **Setting:** Rosaria’s streets, shopping district, and gates. The file includes wartime conversations, an escort request, a family encounter, and dialogue during and after an attack on the capital.
- **Speakers:** Tippi, Luise, Ralph, Sandra, unnamed party members, a military supply transporter, guards, merchants, and citizens. King Alcadius is mentioned as having recognized the hero as a knight.
- **Public reputation and war:** Citizens now praise the party as heroes. They express confidence in Rolandia, Ranzak, and the party against the Imperial Knights, and hope a new shortcut to Nostridge will help end the war. Other dialogue expresses continuing unease even after peace arrives.
- **Disappearances and Luise:** Citizens discuss missing Growsians and warn the hero because his sister is one. Alternative lines acknowledge the incident’s resolution and her safety. A separate travel restriction urges taking the eastern route to Rachelle because Luise has little time.
- **Supply escort:** A transporter cannot deliver supplies to the Nostridge battlefield because monsters repeatedly attack. The front lines are largely surviving on local supplies, and no escort soldiers remain. The party can accept or decline escort duty; companions note that advancing the front is also necessary to reach their destination near a lake.
- **Ralph’s claim:** Ralph introduces himself as someone searching for a twin brother separated from him shortly after birth. Companions remark on his striking resemblance to the hero, and Luise confirms the hero was adopted. Ralph invites them to his inn to talk. This excerpt presents his claim without independently verifying it.
- **Capital under attack:** Monsters rampage in the eastern district, gates close, and guards defend the city. Afterward, the party discusses the successful protection of civilians and whether knowledge of Luise’s weakened Grosh or lost power prompted the attack. Sandra accompanies them home to hear their report.
- **Type and tone:** Main-story developments and urban battle aftermath, plus an escort request, a family encounter, and routine town/system text. Public admiration and reunion excitement alternate with wartime hardship and alarm; Tippi’s jokes and shopkeeper banter continue throughout.
