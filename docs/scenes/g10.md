## s040 - Luise’s Hospitalization and Recovery

- **Setting and speakers:** Rachelle’s convalescent facility, including its lobby, sickrooms, and medical office. Principal speakers include Tippi, Luise, Wallace, Xenos, Karen, Berger, Queen Stella, a Featherian attendant, and medical staff.

- An attack threatens Luise. Its aftermath identifies Gevel as responsible for damage to the building and says the amnesiac patient fought the monster. That patient is revealed as Berger, Karen and Xenos’s missing father and Wallace’s former commander. He is seriously wounded; separate NPC dialogue also mentions his death.

Wallace inadvertently reveals that the hero was created from Berger, then apologizes. Another dialogue passage describes shock at discovering that a brother is not a biological brother, without explicitly identifying its speaker.
- Beside Luise’s bed, Tippi explains that she was created as a homunculus to watch over the siblings. Their treatment of her as a person made her happy; she would sacrifice her life for Luise, but feels powerless. She offers to keep the hero’s tears secret.
- Luise awakens and declares that she has finally made her brother cry. Her Promise Pendant has broken, and the characters credit its wish-granting power with her recovery. She wants the Magic Academy to examine it in case it can also help Irene and others.
- In another sequence while Luise remains incapacitated, Stella says the Featherians cannot restore Grosh. She reveals that they originally created Gevel as a biological weapon against hostile Growsians, who captured and modified it. The Featherians subsequently abandoned life on the surface. Stella acknowledges partial responsibility but offers information rather than direct intervention.
- **Type and tone:** Primarily major story events, with anxious caregiving, painful family revelations, intimate consolation, and joyful relief. It also contains hospital NPC dialogue, Karen recruitment/departure menus, and an event label about examining the pendant.

- An approaching threat interrupts the confrontation. The frightened citizens initially despair, but Tippi tells them to use their weapons and follow the hero’s commands. Subsequent dialogue confirms that fighting has given them confidence.

- **Type and tone:** A substantial story event with a battle lead-in and aftermath, alongside routine town and party-management text. Its tone shifts from accusation and resignation to solidarity, practical responsibility, and encouragement.
