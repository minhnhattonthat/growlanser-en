- **Where/type:** The Magic Academy’s public floors, offices, rooftop, and laboratories. Substantial main-story material and a curse-removal quest are mixed with recurring NPC chatter, library texts, and service menus.
- **Speakers:** Tippi, Luise, Ariost, Vice-Headmaster Bradley, the unnamed mana line engineer, academy staff and students, and other party voices whose names are not attached to their lines.

Delighted that his forty years of experience are needed again, the engineer joins as a companion to install mana lines.
- **Searching for a cure:** Bradley studies Maxwell’s records to help Irene, but finds instructions for extracting Grosh rather than restoring it. Traditions suggest that a Growsian’s innate power is incomplete and can awaken to more than twice its strength; the clues connect this awakening with Growsian births and distortions in space-time.
- **Luise and the attack:** The party reveals that Luise has also lost her Grosh. Her comparatively stable condition is tentatively attributed to her birth during a total solar eclipse. The discussion is interrupted by a message that masked enemies have surrounded the royal capital, prompting an immediate return.
- **The broken pendant:** In another exchange, Luise has recovered and reports that her Grosh returned when the Promise Pendant broke. Bradley agrees to examine it and connects her recovery with a recorded distortion to the north. The party also reports that “true Grosh” probably exists, describing heightened perception; the pendant’s role remains unproven here.
- **Removing Karen’s curse:** Bradley explains a curse that lets its caster inflict suffering or death remotely through a keyword. He requests hair or nails and holy soil, which forms where Grosh seeps into the ground without collection pillars intercepting it. The party delivers Karen’s hair and the soil; he prepares a substitute doll and later joins them because the ritual requires his expertise.
- **Tone:** Urgent concern for afflicted companions and the capital alternates with academic curiosity, Bradley’s boastful humor, and the engineer’s enthusiasm. Routine academy dialogue includes reports of masked intruders and unease over unethical research.

## s034 - The Hero’s Power and Its Cost

- **Where/type:** The Magic Academy, especially Bradley’s office, the rooftop observatory, and Maxwell’s former laboratory. Predominantly major story revelations, with an attack setup, a playable concentration sequence, optional exploration lore, and ordinary menus.
- **Speakers:** Bradley, Tippi, Luise, Ariost, Irene, academy students and staff, and additional unnamed party voices. Bradley has formally become headmaster and moved into Maxwell’s office.
- **Wenzel’s identity:** The party reports that Wenzel is a fusion of a Growsian and Gevel, possessing Gevel’s power and control over space-time. A device controlling the Power Stone removed his rapid regeneration, but his teleportation and destructive magic remain threats. A report that a monster is approaching interrupts the meeting; Bradley asks the party to rescue students already fighting.
- **A failing world:** Rooftop observations indicate that the overlapping worlds are separating. Humanity originally survived its uninhabitable world by overlapping it with this one; continued separation would cause human extinction within two years. The measuring instrument is confirmed to be functioning.
- **Reconsidering Luise’s recovery:** Bradley finds that the Promise Pendant has no restorative effect. Tippi explains that Luise’s vow concerned making the hero cry. Bradley proposes that the emotional state accompanying those tears, rather than the tears themselves, triggered an unconscious ability.
- **Experiment and revelation:** With Irene taking Luise’s place, the hero attempts to recreate that emotional state through a button-mashing concentration sequence. He produces a distortion and loses consciousness; Irene recovers, and Bradley becomes a Growsian. Dialogue identifies the hero as a human replica created by Gevel whose existence belongs to both worlds, explaining his strength and space-time ability. Luise confirms that her older brother also restored her.
- **The cost:** Creating more Growsians could provide enough people to make a Power Stone and operate a device that prevents separation. However, each use of the hero’s ability weakens the worlds’ overlap. Bradley estimates that the hero can endure only one or two further uses and warns that the worlds might separate before enough Growsians are created.
- **Other material/tone:** Students and professors search old literature for a way to defeat Wenzel. An unfinished book describes a changing Featherian underground cave, more than ten levels deep, accessible through a custodian’s family in Schutzberg. The prevailing tone moves from scholarly determination and affectionate encouragement to discovery, hope, and a severe dilemma.
