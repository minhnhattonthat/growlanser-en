## s120 - Records and the Final Battle

- **Where/type:** Growsian records room, Power Stone refining room, and space-time control apparatus; main-story climax with battle dialogue, terminal readouts, and party-selection menus.
- **Speakers:** Wenzel, Xenos, Tippi, Luise, and other party members; an automated warning system also speaks. Party options name Ariost, Misha, Karen, Elliot, and Julian. Many individual lines lack speaker labels.
- Records explain that humans and Featherians jointly developed space-time control to escape their troubled homeworld. Power Stones solved the resulting spatial distortion. Growsian plans then called for seizing the apparatus, converting its floating platform into their stronghold, and appropriating Gevel.
- Gevel was a Featherian biological weapon derived from the native creature Gev. The records describe modifying it so it could not exercise its power in the presence of Grosh, and theorize that beings connected to both worlds could manipulate space-time and pass that ability to descendants.
- Berger, a commoner rebel designated **Berger=K**, survived enhancement experiments with extraordinary combat strength and incomplete space-time powers. He escaped execution with a stolen Power Stone. Applying the experiment to Wenzel caused rejection; the remaining record is corrupted.
- The party identifies Berger’s biological son, **Xenos**, as another bearer of the needed ability. Richard has been killed by Wenzel, but Xenos can help the hero make everyone Growsian and gather the power needed to create a Power Stone. The party reunites, chooses six participants, and leaves others to secure an escape route.
- Wenzel challenges them in the refining room and argues that an immortal ruler would prevent recurring struggles for power. At the control apparatus he appears in a monstrous form, explaining that drawing out Gevel’s power cost him his space-time ability and leaves him at most one day to live. He intends to take the party and world with him.
- **Outcome/tone:** Urgent revelations, renewed hope, and a desperate final confrontation. Alarms warn that separation begins above 35% distortion; a failure line says they were too late. The success branch defeats Wenzel, uses the Power Stone, reduces distortion to 0.6%, and has the relieved party return to their waiting companions.
