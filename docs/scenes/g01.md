## s004 - Rosaria under threat

- **Setting/type:** Rosaria, Rolandia’s royal capital: castle gates, streets, shops, and the magic classroom. This file mixes main-story events, town conversations, shop menus, and battle-related dialogue across several story states.
- **Speakers:** Citizens, gatekeepers, merchants, children, soldiers, recruits, an LLM magic instructor, and unnamed party voices. Sandra, Richard, and Ernest—also addressed as Lyell—appear in the confrontation.
- A grieving mother blames a castle knight for angering Wenzel and causing her guard son’s death during a Gevel-type monster attack. A guard urges the party not to blame themselves.
- Everyday dialogue covers shopping, dating, childhood memories, magic lessons, and recruitment. Hidden-shop discoveries and joking manhole descriptions provide comic relief; other variants show loneliness, bereavement, and wartime disruption.
- The party discusses Wenzel’s ability to teleport people from a distance. They say defeating him alone will not end the world’s danger: cooperation involving Richard remains necessary, and they discuss alternatives if persuasion fails. Ariost’s mother is mentioned as a potential ally.
- During an attack, Sandra rallies the defenders against the Imperial Knights. The party recognizes Richard and Lyell; the attackers declare that they will kill Rolandians who obstruct Wenzel.
- Aftermath branches report either civilian safety or some casualties. Companions worry about the hero’s physical condition, urge medical attention at the sanatorium, and promise support. Other lines concern gathering for a party and arranging teleportation to Burnstein.
- **Tone:** Ordinary urban humor and warmth alternate with grief, intimidation, guilt, and determined solidarity. The menus and interaction restrictions are gameplay text; nothing clearly identifies debug material.

## s005 - The empty hall and the red earring

- **Setting/type:** A multipurpose hall associated with the Magic Academy, where Growsians were supposed to be protected. Primarily a main-story investigation, with deduction choices and exploration prompts.
- **Speakers:** Unnamed party members and Luise, who is directly addressed and calls the hero her older brother. Irene is the person they expect to find; the academy’s headmaster and vice-headmaster are discussed by title.
- The hall is unnervingly quiet. Irene and the other Growsians are absent, and the party discovers a dead man whom one companion recognizes as an acquaintance of the vice-headmaster.
- The corpse holds a red ruby earring. Luise is reluctantly pressed to retrieve it, producing a frightened comic exchange amid the investigation.
- Player choices suggest several suspects or explanations, including academy officials, the dead man’s associates, the earring’s owner, or the Growsians themselves. These remain hypotheses: the party explicitly concludes that there is insufficient evidence and keeps the earring.
- Exploration text reveals an unusual wall that opens onto a descending passage and a door farther along. Other lines warn against using magic inside academy facilities and note that no invisibility medicine remains.
- **Tone:** Suspenseful and investigative, interrupted by nervous humor. Neither the killer nor the missing Growsians’ fate is established here.

## s006 - Royal service and the first missions

- **Setting/type:** Rolandia’s royal castle, including the audience chamber, kitchens, guarded rooms, treasury, and prison. Mainly main-story mission briefings and reports, with incidental conversations and leave-selection menus.
- **Speakers:** King Alcadius, Sandra, Princess Leticia, Luise, Wallace, Misha, unnamed companions, soldiers, cooks, and officials. Misha’s anxious introduction to Leticia supplies a comic contrast to court formality.
- Sandra presents the hero and Luise as her children. She explains that the hero’s dreams reveal real distant events and saved her life; a monster resembling the legendary Gevel has appeared in them. Luise is identified as a Growsian, and Wallace is proposed as their experienced support.
- Alcadius accepts their service and assigns them to escort his daughter Leticia to a rendezvous beyond the southern fort. Burnstein’s Imperial Knights will take over for its prince’s coronation; the party must later collect her again.
- The Gevel investigation draws on Wallace’s account of losing his captain and subordinates at a crystal mine about twenty years earlier, and his later injury near Crane. Connections between these incidents, masked attackers, and Gevel are presented as possibilities requiring investigation.
- A mine report suggests that monsters may have emerged from inside crystal. Sandra arranges an academy investigation; a subsequent briefing says a collapse blocked the passage when the expedition entered. The headmaster and vice-headmaster participated, but there were no deaths.
- Another audience reveals war and Leticia’s absence. Shocked by news involving Lord Greg, Alcadius orders the party to prioritize rescuing hostages, consult General Bronson, convey reinforcement plans, and obstruct enemy movements until reinforcements arrive. Luise’s teleportation offers a return route.
- **Tone and systems:** Formal duty, growing danger, and family concern coexist with castle humor. Mission rewards introduce one-to-three-day leave and destination selection, explicitly intended partly to strengthen companions’ relationships.

## s007 - Knighthood and a diplomatic mission

- **Setting/type:** Rolandia’s royal castle. Main-story aftermath of Leticia’s rescue, followed by diplomatic planning; repeated castle conversations and leave menus also appear.
- **Speakers:** Alcadius, Leticia, Sandra, Elliot, Wallace, Luise, Misha, unnamed companions, and castle staff.
- Leticia confirms that Lord Greg attacked King Richard, but says he seemed like a different person despite looking like Greg. This is her impression, not confirmation of an impersonation.
- Elliot explains that his father sent him to the king with a letter, which he now suspects was stolen during an inn attack. He has learned that his parents are not his birth parents; they often mentioned Wenzel. His resemblance to Richard and matching bracelet prompt investigation into a possible connection.
- Sandra identifies Wenzel as Burnstein’s chief court magician and her teacher. She reports that he has disappeared. Alcadius keeps Elliot at the castle while the circumstances remain unresolved.
- Alcadius rewards the hero for rescuing Leticia and preventing an invasion with knighthood and land outside Rosaria’s west gate. Companions celebrate; an incidental exchange jokingly compares Wallace’s admonishing manner to a father’s.
- Concern over a possible two-front war leads to a proposed alliance with Ranzak. After leave, the party must secretly seek a nonaggression agreement. Merchant papers permit travel as far as Galashiels; onward access is left to their judgment, with Luise’s teleportation helping once locations have been visited.
- **Tone:** Relief and celebration give way to political uncertainty and strategic urgency. Choice menus explore competing responses to the military threat; they do not establish every proposed action as something that happens.

## s008 - Alliances and the Growsian disappearances

- **Setting/type:** Rolandia’s royal castle during wartime, including audiences and a supply convoy’s departure. Main-story reports and assignments are mixed with optional character conversations, castle chatter, and leave menus.
- **Speakers:** Alcadius, Sandra, Wallace, Luise, Tippi, Misha, Karen, Leticia, unnamed companions, soldiers, transport personnel, cooks, and officials. A male speaker asks about wanting brothers, but this exchange does not name him.
- Several briefings concern the Ranzak alliance. The party reports activating ruins in the Lost Forest to provoke fighting between Burnstein and Ranzak, breaking their alliance. Alcadius then sends official envoys to Ranzak; a later report confirms Rolandia’s alliance and credits the fighting with buying defensive preparation time.
- The party resumes its Gevel investigation using the hero’s dreams and the location where Wallace lost an eye and an arm. Sandra suggests investigating the nearby village and using Luise’s teleportation to reach previously visited Garuaos Prison.
- A subsequent report describes a breeding site behind a Burnstein waterfall, where captured humans fed small creatures called Jung. The party destroyed all the eggs there, but did not find Gevel. They also report Growsian disappearances, suspicious deaths, and the murder of a Growsian village chief; Rolandia has experienced similar disappearances.
- Alcadius begins protecting Growsians and assigns the party to locate Irene, a sanatorium worker, and escort her to the Magic Academy. Karen knows her and is described as her beneficiary. The file also contains a successful delivery report.
- Another dream shows abundant Grosh and Jung around a clear lake, identified in discussion as Lake Olivier. A newly completed military supply road provides an approach, but Burnstein’s deployment near the lake means the front must be pushed back. The convoy carries supplies to troops at Nostridge.
- **Relationships/tone:** Optional exchanges tease the hero about Luise and discuss sibling companionship. Misha recalls her birthplace, Medis, and receiving lilies from the academy headmaster, though dialogue disputes her flower-field memory. Warm banter and domestic humor sit alongside military resolve and disturbing reports of human victims.
