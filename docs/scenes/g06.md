## s026 - The Academy Under Attack

- **Setting and type:** The Magic Academy, including Ariost’s laboratory and the multipurpose hall. This file combines ordinary campus dialogue, main-story crises, battle dialogue, and party-management prompts.
- **Speakers:** Wenzel, Wallace, Ariost, Misha, Tippi, Luise, the Featherian queen, Ariost’s mother Gina, Julian, and unnamed students, professors, guards, Featherians, and Burnstein soldiers.
- Students discuss research opportunities, exhausting coursework, scarce magic crystals, and doubts about their abilities. Later dialogue shifts to fear of an attack and possible separation of the world’s overlapping dimensions; abnormal readings send attention to the rooftop distortion instrument.
- Wenzel declares that knowledgeable people threaten a ruler and orders the Academy’s personnel exterminated. Students struggle to defend themselves; Misha angrily defends her school and friends, while Ariost calls the Academy his workplace.
- After the fighting, the party discusses orders to kill people but preserve research. They interpret Wenzel’s attacks as eliminating potential military opposition while retaining research and civilian labor. Survivors express gratitude and lingering fear.
- In another major sequence, Wenzel announces that he has lost teleportation but still possesses a Growsian weapon: a floating castle combining Featherian and Growsian construction. Luise explains that her teleportation requires fixed terrain and an unobstructed arrival point, making the moving castle inaccessible by that method.
- Gina and the Featherian queen explain that disabling their levitation technology will bring down both the castle and Featherland. They credit the party with changing their view of humanity, and humanity’s united determination with stripping Wenzel’s teleportation. Featherians evacuate, the castle descends, and the party prepares for the final battle near Fort Rajin.
- **Tone and supporting text:** Campus banter gives way to terror, defiance, sacrifice, and hope. Recruitment options cover Julian, Misha, and Ariost; other prompts handle equipment recovery, locked doors, and restrictions on indoor magic.

## s027 - Meeting Misha and Seeking Ariost

- **Setting and type:** The Magic Academy’s cafeteria, classrooms, library, restricted reading room, pharmaceutical floor, administrative offices, rooftop, and basement laboratories. Primarily main-story introductions and exploration, with optional lore, side-event hooks, shops, and navigation text.
- **Speakers:** Tippi, Luise, Wallace, Misha, Headmaster Maxwell, students, professors, cafeteria and shop staff, a librarian, and administrators’ secretaries. Ariost, Sandra, and Vice-Headmaster Bradley are discussed.
- While the party searches for Ariost, Misha collides with the hero and becomes excitedly infatuated with him. Luise introduces Misha as her friend and the hero as her brother; Misha immediately asks to call him “Onii-sama.” She reports that Ariost hurried to the southern Featherian ruins seeking something needed to finish his work.
- The party requests permission to enter those ruins. Maxwell recognizes Luise and asks about her practical training under Sandra. He grabs and examines Tippi, identifying her as Sandra’s homunculus; Tippi protests and kicks him. Maxwell nevertheless gives them the permit.
- Rooftop dialogue explains that two forcibly overlapping space-times sustain their world. Separation would return people to their original world, rendered deadly by an abnormal sun. The Academy therefore continually measures distortion.
- Library books describe Growsians’ eclipse-linked access to their original world’s magical substance, Growshu, and their exceptional teleportation. Other texts cover the unusually powerful crystal mine, the unresolved war and disappearances a century earlier, and differences between Growsian and Featherian ruins.
- Optional conversations include an invisibility-drug project needing Kuriano grass from Mount Buremu, suspicions about the cafeteria meal, and Bradley’s secretary defending his curse research as work to help victims. Professors also explain combat timing, interrupted casting, and item recovery delays.
- **Tone:** Mostly lively campus comedy—Misha’s crush, Tippi’s temper, and teasing about an attractive secretary—with serious worldbuilding beneath it. Elevator menus, purchases, permits, and magic-use warnings are ordinary system text; the combat advice is instructional rather than an actual battle.

## s028 - Featherian Research and Restricted Flight

- **Setting and type:** Another Academy script covering the same floors, especially its libraries and Maxwell’s office. It mixes main-story investigation and Ariost’s research dispute with an optional Misha encounter, campus chatter, lore, and system menus.
- **Speakers:** Ariost, Wallace, Tippi, Luise, Misha, Maxwell, students, professors, librarians, shop and cafeteria staff, and secretaries. Bradley is discussed; Danny Gureizu is identified as a book’s author.
- The party investigates Featherians. Ariost leaves the library search to his companions while investigating his parents: he argues that his own birth challenges the idea that Featherians simply look down on humans. He asks the party to bring discoveries to his laboratory.
- The ordinary library proves insufficient, so the party obtains Maxwell’s permission to consult restricted Featherian material. A book describes Featherians as winged, highly rational beings with knowledge exceeding even the Growsians at their height, but leaves the party’s questions unanswered.
- They decide to seek the author, Danny Gureizu, in Comsprings, a Burnstein hot-spring town. Crossing the border requires permission. A separate reception exchange mentions requesting a Comsprings pass after winning the fighting tournament, but Maxwell is then in a meeting.
- An optional library encounter reveals Misha trapped beneath fallen books and asleep after exhausting herself trying to escape. Clearing the books frees her; alternatives include leaving them alone or proposing to burn them, prompting objections. Her affection for the hero and friendship with Luise continue the comic tone.
- Newly available books report a monster incident and fatal casualties at the crystal mine, an investigation obstructed by a cave-in, a changing underground cave whose custodian was found in Shuttsuberugu, and a Growsian device responsible for the Lost Forest’s disorientation. These are presented as written reports or legends.
- Maxwell impounds Ariost’s successful flying apparatus and research under magical-technology regulations because of potential military use, providing the prescribed reward. Ariost says research is his “proof,” but postpones explaining until they visit the village house where he lived with his parents. **Tone:** Curious investigation and slapstick shift toward disappointment and a personal family discussion; there is combat advice, but no battle sequence.
