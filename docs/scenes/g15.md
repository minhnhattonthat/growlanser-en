## s067 - Building the Hero’s Town

- **Where/type:** The player-named hero’s estate, developed into a town. Primarily a town-building side activity, with leisure events, facility menus, and optional dungeon instructions.
- **Speakers:** The administrator, Tippi, Misha, Karen, Luise, Wallace, Dungeon Man, soldiers, visitors, customers, and a boat attendant.
- The administrator explains that the hero chooses and funds facilities; he arranges construction, while operating income covers staffing. Available facilities include a theater, exhibition hall, restaurants, and a park whose initial construction the king funds.
- The companions propose town names: Misha suggests flower names associated with joy and a bright future; Karen suggests “Growland”; Luise offers playful names; Wallace suggests the hero’s name; Tippi proposes “Tippi-chan Kingdom.” The player can choose a suggestion or supply a name, with later renaming available for a fee.
- Development involves recruiting performers, artists, and cooks. Visitors discuss the Johnny Quartet, Barbara’s opera, the troupe *Sangoku Tairiku*, and exhibitions by Adal and Mana.
- The park includes a couple who met there and an optional boathouse. Party dialogue reserves boating, dining, and attending performances for vacation rather than working hours.
- Dungeon Man introduces a randomized, fourteen-floor dungeon with unpredictable treasure and monster encounters. He refuses entry with an accompanying escort character because that person’s defeat would cause a game over. The overall tone is cheerful, teasing, and recreational.

## s068 - Meetings at the Western Cape

- **Where/type:** The Western Cape, overlooking the Featherians’ floating island, with a nearby grave and dialogue on the journey home. The file combines main-story exposition and travel preparations with a mysterious personal encounter.
- **Speakers:** Tippi, Ariost—initially labeled “young man”—Luise, Wallace, and a mysterious woman who identifies herself as Sierra. The hero has selectable responses.
- Ariost admires Featherian wings and explains his ambition to reach their island through flight research at the Magic Academy. He recognizes Tippi as a homunculus; she introduces herself and identifies Luise as the hero’s sister.
- Ariost explains that people born during solar or lunar eclipses acquire special magical power called Grosh and are called Growsians. Luise, born during a total solar eclipse, is exceptionally powerful. Grosh also appears as drifting lights entering the world through unstable space-time, becoming more plentiful toward the north.
- Sierra says she has waited twenty years for the person she addresses. She “returns” Sierra’s Ring and asks that her child be told that their mother still loves them. Tippi identifies her as someone from the hero’s dream; a grave records that Sierra died three years before the hero’s birth. The excerpt does not resolve whom Sierra believes she is addressing.
- In the antidote mission, Ariost’s flying device can carry him and one additional passenger using a supplementary belt. Luise must go first so that, once she knows the destination, her teleportation can take the whole party there.
- Departure depends on Luise learning Teleport. She fears the flight after an earlier malfunction, while Ariost insists he has corrected the problem. The hero can encourage her, invoke Sandra’s need, or make a grim joke that earns Tippi’s kick.
- The tone moves between scientific curiosity, affectionate comedy, urgent concern for Sandra, and an eerie, sorrowful encounter with Sierra.

## s069 - The Secret Laboratory’s Revelations

- **Where/type:** A secret laboratory reached through a concealed cave passage. A major main-story confrontation, with exploration puzzles, battle-related dialogue, and an emotional aftermath.
- **Speakers:** Irene, Vice-Headmaster Bradley, the unnamed Academy headmaster and his secretary, Tippi, Luise, Misha, Karen, other companions, and an unnamed man personally attached to Irene.
- Access puzzles concern details of the Magic Academy and a three-digit code supported by book clues. Books cover homunculi, eclipses, space-time, Growsian ruins, and related research.
- The party interrupts an operation attacking Irene’s memories. Another captive warns that it will leave her profoundly incapacitated. She survives, but the aftermath depicts severe damage and the party’s horror at research conducted through such cruelty.
- The headmaster declares that he descends from the ancient Growsian royal ruling class. He claims interbreeding with ordinary humans ended his lineage’s Grosh abilities, and intends to regain those abilities and restore Growsian domination.
- He reveals that both his secretary and Misha are human-sized homunculi. Misha was created to approach and monitor Luise, whose Grosh he covets, while believing herself human. He also asks how Misha’s telepathic connection was blocked.
- Luise fiercely defends Misha and later insists that their shared happiness and friendship were real. Misha’s distress gives way to an emotional reaffirmation of their bond; another companion stresses trust and recalls his father’s refusal to abandon someone.
- Tippi goes outside the magic jammer’s range to contact her master and arrange official assistance. The tone is sinister and confrontational, then grief-stricken and consoling.

## s070 - Rescuing Stella and Uncovering Gevel’s Origins

- **Where/type:** The Growsian ruins, across multiple event sequences. Main-story dungeon material combining rescue, puzzles, timed danger, combat, and historical revelations.
- **Speakers:** Ariost, Luise, Tippi, Wallace and other companions, Featherian Queen Stella, hostile creatures, an automated alarm, and passages read from a diary and report.
- Following a faint, sorrowful song, the party seeks the captive Featherian queen. Ariost explains that the Academy’s locked entrance is an artificial breach; Luise can open the original entrance because it responds to Growsians.
- Stella initially mistakes the rescuers for allies of her captors. She accepts that her people will not endanger themselves for her, prompting debate about acceptance and surrender. Ariost reveals that his mother, Gina, was a Featherian who had a child with a human, and quotes his father’s teaching never to abandon hope.
- Stella’s injured wings prevent flight, so she joins as an escort. Outside, she recalls a one-eyed leader among her captors and says she was imprisoned for refusing to disclose Featherian technology that humans might use for warfare.
- Dungeon obstacles include a four-button sequence selected from five colors, inscribed clues, locked passages, and a countdown to floor demolition. Another sequence finds creatures smashing doors and their contents; the party intervenes to preserve whatever remains.
- Surviving records describe ancient Growsian rulers using Gevel biological weapons against ordinary humans, while some Growsians sided with humanity. A technical report says the existing Gevel model cannot cast magic and has reduced magical resistance because of safeguards against harming Growsians, although ordinary human magic remains insufficient.
- The records propose an improved model, but the party cannot determine which model they currently face. They resolve to report to the king. The tone combines urgency, ideological conflict, and disturbing discoveries.

## s071 - Ariost’s Missing Propulsion Device

- **Where/type:** The Featherian ruins, guarded under Academy authority. Primarily a main-story rescue and equipment-retrieval event, with exploration text and an optional cave-access clue.
- **Speakers:** Ariost, Luise, Tippi, Wallace and other companions, and a guard who checks the headmaster’s authorization.
- The party enters looking for Ariost and finds him in mortal danger. After being rescued, he leads them deeper inside to the object he came to retrieve.
- Luise admits this is her first visit despite studying the ruins. Asked to read the books, she becomes embarrassed and tearful because she cannot decipher Featherian writing. Ariost explains that most of the books concern ethics and rules of conduct.
- Ariost recounts traditions of ancient Growsian flying towers using Featherian technology. His own floating disk can rise but depends on winds for movement, making it unsuitable for reliably reaching the high island.
- He finds a propulsion device that should let his flying apparatus travel against the wind. Luise explains that her mother has been poisoned and needs an antidote from the Featherians; Ariost agrees to transport them in gratitude for his rescue and joins as an escort.
- Optional reading describes a Featherian-created “Evolution Cave” managed by a human. Its access passwords are **“boketsu”** and **“shishi.”** Other shelves concern etiquette, child-rearing, and successive Featherian queens.
- The tone mixes adventurous discovery and gentle comedy with Luise’s urgent fear for her mother. A transition label and reminder direct the party to Ariost’s laboratory to install the device.

## s072 - Fort Rajin and the Outbreak of War

- **Where/type:** Fort Rajin and the nearby eastern rendezvous plain. Multiple main-story visits cover escort duty, war, and rescue preparations, alongside merchant services and local information.
- **Speakers:** Princess Leticia, General Bronson, Imperial Knight Ernest Lyell, Wallace, Tippi, Luise, other companions, guards, soldiers, a merchant, and an unnamed returning attacker.
- Wallace explains that the fort guards the junction of Rolandia, Burnstein, and Ranzak. It also watches nearby forest ruins containing an inactive Growsian device once used to disrupt human direction-finding.
- While awaiting her onward escort, Leticia asks the girls to become her friends and describes her excitement at finally seeing the world outside the castle. An attacker previously associated with targeting Karen tries to injure Leticia so her escorts will be disgraced or executed.
- Ernest Lyell introduces himself and takes responsibility for escorting Leticia to Burnstein’s coronation. Other visit dialogue depicts the party returning with her safely, receiving Bronson’s praise, resting, and being instructed to escort her home.
- A messenger announces Burnstein’s declaration of war, reporting that Lord Greg attacked King Richard during the coronation. Rolandia’s delegation, including Leticia, has been captured. Bronson orders intelligence gathering and sends the party to the king with a request for reinforcements.
- Burnstein begins bridging a canyon to shorten its advance by a week. A subsequent branch records that the party defeated the bridge builders and destroyed the bridge. Intelligence then places Leticia in transit to Garuaos Prison, whose terrain makes rescue after arrival exceptionally difficult.
- The destroyed bridge also obstructs the rescuers. Bronson defends their earlier decision and orders a rope-based crossing operation; Luise’s teleportation is proposed for the return journey. The tone shifts from friendly royal companionship to wartime alarm and urgent action, with brief comedy over the ruined route.
