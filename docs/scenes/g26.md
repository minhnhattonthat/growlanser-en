## s258 - Karen’s Conflicted Affections

- **Where / speakers:** Several encounters around Karen’s work and a flower field where she gathers medicinal herbs; no town is named. Karen, Tippi, Xenos, and Irene speak with the hero.
- Irene embarrasses Karen by calling the hero her boyfriend. Karen denies it, admits loving someone, and describes that love as permanently unrequited; Irene’s persistent questioning drives her away.
- Karen carries a picture of Xenos and once mistakenly calls the hero “Brother.” Tippi recalls that Karen and Xenos have supported each other as their only two siblings.
- Xenos becomes fiercely protective when the hero visits Karen. Karen rejects his attempts to control her, insisting she can make her own decisions; Xenos realizes he went too far.
- After a rescue, Karen thanks the party and reports that Xenos says he has been freed from the Shadow Knight. She then confesses that she had been romantically in love with her brother.
- Karen explains that her feelings shifted toward the hero. Seeing him arrive in the dark dungeon made her feel safe; she credits him with saving her emotionally as well as physically.
- Another exchange shows her distressed because the hero has her father’s appearance and blood. She questions whether she loves him or merely sees her father in him, then apologizes and recognizes that he has his own identity.
- **Type / tone:** Romance and family side events tied to story revelations. The tone moves from comic embarrassment and sibling friction to vulnerable confession, anguish, and acceptance.

## s260 - Julian Reveals Julia

- **Where / speakers:** A hot-spring destination and its inn. Tippi says the territory from there to the crystal mine falls under Magic Academy jurisdiction. Julian, Tippi, and an unnamed doctor speak with the hero.
- Julian confronts the visiting party because their countries are at war. Tippi insists they are simply enjoying leave and argues that Academy jurisdiction makes the visit acceptable.
- During another encounter, Julian collapses. The hero carries Julian to the inn while Tippi fetches a doctor, who diagnoses insufficient sleep and reveals that the patient is a woman.
- Tippi insists they preserve Julian’s secret out of respect for the effort required to become an Imperial Knight. If the hero persists in wanting to tell others, she kicks him.
- Julian later explains that Imperial Knights must be men; exposure would cost her title. She asks why the hero did not exploit this advantage, and receives answers emphasizing fairness or personal regard.
- Julia Douglas is her real name. Her family has produced many Imperial Knights; she began sword training voluntarily because she believed she was responsible for her father’s disappointment at having a daughter.
- She recounts losing her father’s attention to her younger brother. Even after surpassing him, she received disappointment rather than praise and left home. She still blames being born female, but says meeting the hero has eased her distress.
- **Type / tone:** Character side events with major identity and family revelations. Hostile rivalry develops into trust, gratitude, and painful disclosure; Julian initially still anticipates settling their rivalry on the battlefield.

## s261 - Xenos’s Family and Cooking

- **Where / speakers:** An unnamed place where Xenos is spending time before a job begins. Xenos and Tippi speak with the hero; a possible visit to Xenos’s home in Grancil is discussed.
- Xenos thanks the party for their earlier help and for visiting Karen. He says she is doing well but refuses to discuss the details of his work.
- He explains that their father disappeared during his childhood. Their mother raised both children alone and eventually died from overwork.
- Xenos became a mercenary using the physical strength he says he inherited from his father. He insists he will protect Karen and responds awkwardly but appreciatively when the hero expresses concern for him.
- Another conversation reveals that Xenos reads during long battlefield waits—and that his current book is a cookbook. He offers to cook for the party.
- One branch turns that offer into an immediate invitation home. He tells the hero to fetch Luise so they can use teleportation to reach Grancil.
- **Type / tone:** Character and family side events, combining bereavement and sibling responsibility with friendly humor about Xenos’s unexpected interest in cooking.
